Cancellation & Refund

Customers may request cancellation before an order is dispatched. Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may not be possible.

If a product arrives damaged, incorrect, or materially different from the order, contact Millet Mitra promptly with order details and supporting photographs where applicable.

Approved refunds for online payments will be processed through the applicable payment method/provider. COD orders are not charged until delivery unless otherwise stated.

Requests are reviewed case by case based on product condition and order status.
